# 10 Ethics and Privacy

In this block we cover:

* Ethical data science
  * The law: Human Rights Act, GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018
  * GDPR and its implications for data science
  * Data ownership
* Statistical disclosure
  * Anonymity and re-identification (Governor Weld, the Netflix prize)
  * Statistical disclosure attacks
  * Protecting against and quantifying vulnerability to disclosure
  * Differential privacy, the Laplace mechanism and randomized response
  * Practice for sharing anonymised data
* Interpretability and fairness
  * What is interpretability?
  * Interpretable algorithms vs interpreting black box algorithms
  * Algorithmic fairness: counterfactuals and proxy data
  * Measures of algorithmic fairness
* Discussions
  * Opting out of NHS data
  * Privacy in the census
  * Interpretable models or black boxes?

### The law

* [EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR 2018)](https://eugdpr.org)
* [Data Protection Act 2018 (DPA 2018)](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/guide-to-data-protection/)
* [KDNuggets](https://www.kdnuggets.com/2017/07/gdpr-affects-data-science.html) has a very good description of how GDPR affects Data Science.

### Statistical disclosure and privacy

* Dwork and Roth 2014 [The Algorithmic Foundations of Differential Privacy](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~aaroth/Papers/privacybook.pdf).
* [ONS](https://www.ons.gov.uk/methodology/methodologytopicsandstatisticalconcepts/disclosurecontrol) policy on disclosure control.
* [Sweeney 1997](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1748-720X.1997.tb01885.x). Weaving technology and policy together to maintain confidentiality. Journal of Law, Medicine & Ethics, 25:98–110.
